1. What goes in the <head> of a page versus the <body>, and who sees each?
    The <head> has page information. The <body> has what people see on the page.
2. What is the difference between running your page at localhost:3000 and at <your-IP>:8080?
    localhost:3000 runs on my computer. <your-IP>:8080 runs online from the droplet.
3. In your own words, what does push do and what does pull do in today's loop?
    Push sends my code to GitHub. Pull gets the newest code from GitHub.
4. Why do we test on localhost:3000 before pushing to production?
    We test locally to make sure everything works before putting it online.
