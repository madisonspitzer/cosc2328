1. What is the difference between an unordered (<ul>) and an ordered (<ol>) list, and when would you use each?
    <ul> uses bullets. <ol> uses numbers. Use <ol> when order matters.
2. Why is a relative link the right choice for a page on your own server, and when must you use a full absolute URL (starting with https://)? (In ../ic3/ic3.html, what does .. mean?)
    A relative link is for pages on your own site. A full https:// link is for outside websites. .. means go back one folder.
3. What is alt text on an image for, and who benefits from it?
    It describes the image for screen readers and if the image does not load.
4. What does it mean that images/favorite.jpg is a relative path?
    images/favorite.jpg means the image is inside the images folder near the HTML file.
